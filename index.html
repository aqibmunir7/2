Our story

A village bakery, run by Sarah

The Village Bakery & Sandwich Shop is run by Sarah in Gee Cross, Hyde. It is the kind of local bakery people walk to for a sandwich, a big barm, or a loaf — and come back to for a bespoke cake.

There is no website of its own yet. The shop’s menu, bakes and bespoke-cake enquiries live on Instagram and Facebook, and customers message the shop by inbox.

If you have only seen the bakery on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the address, the hours, and what to ask for when you get there.
